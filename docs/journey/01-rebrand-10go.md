# 01 · Ten, go

**Goal:** pick a direction from two quick mockups.

**What happened**
- Built two small hero mockups: A "The Ledger" (sharp editorial grid) and B "Terrain" (living contour lines + a name that morphs between English and Georgian). `docs/redesign/mockups/directions-a-b.html`
- Tengo liked the style but wanted the top to be more fun, and shared a rebrand: **Tengiz → Tengo**, which can be written **10go**.
- That one pun became the concept. The intro counts 1 to 10, "go" slides in, and the 0 and o turn into eyes that watch your cursor. Then you choose: Tengo at work, or Tengo the person.

**Decisions**
- Homepage is business-first (Jay Baer style: offer, proof, clear call to action).
- A separate "Get to know me" page carries the playful side.
- The 10go eyes are the thread that links both: in the intro, in the logo, and in the "Get to know me" headline.

**Built:** `docs/redesign/mockups/10go-intro.html` (intro, work view, personal view).

**Lessons**
- A personal pun beats an abstract concept. "Layers" was clever; "10go" is memorable.
- The build sandbox couldn't open the reference sites, so the mockup was built from the owner's description rather than side-by-side study.

**Next:** owner feedback on the intro, the eye placement (0/o vs e's), and the business vs personal balance, then M1 foundations.
