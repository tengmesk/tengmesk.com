# 02 · Getting serious

**Goal:** turn the playful 10go concept into a homepage that works for clients, boards and event organisers.

**Feedback that changed things**
- The eyes in the 0 and o were fun but too playful for a business audience. Removed.
- The intro chooser went too. The homepage should simply say who Tengo is, what he's done and where he can help.
- The personal material moves to an About page.
- Light mode. Language choice lives in the footer (and the mobile menu).

**What got built**
- `docs/redesign/mockups/home-professional.html`: a full light homepage with real content from the press kit (Conceptdigital, Boon, Digital Institute, McKinsey agile bank case, Forbes 30 Under 30, Global Tech Weekend photos, interviews).
- A background switcher in the hero to compare five options side by side: contour lines, a dot field, flowing particle lines, a soft aurora gradient, and the Stamba rooftop photo.
- Draft Georgian translation of the whole page behind the language selector.

**Lessons**
- Real assets change the design. One good event photo (the warm Stamba roof) did more for credibility than any animation.
- Letting the owner flip between backgrounds live beats describing them in a list.

**Next:** pick a hero background, confirm role one-liners and timeline order, then start M1 foundations.
