04 — Takes

Five things Tengo will say in the first ten minutes.

  1. AI is underhyped in the back office and overhyped in the boardroom.

    The boring workflows pay back first: invoices, reporting, ops.

  2. Mid-sized companies have the AI advantage now.

    Fewer layers and faster decisions beat bigger budgets.

  3. Build vs buy has flipped.

    With AI, a custom internal tool often costs less than another 50 SaaS seats.

  4. The most expensive thing in tech isn't developers. It's slow decisions.

    Every week a decision waits, the bill runs.

  5. Growth is an engineering problem now.

    The teams that win at go-to-market instrument everything and test weekly.

06 — Off the clock

Born in Manchester to a Georgian family. Sold sweets in the playground to fund an expedition to Argentina.

Tengo is the Georgian short form of Tengiz. He grew up in Manchester idolising Silicon Valley, and the sweets paid for a World Challenge expedition to Argentina. The business bug never left.

Engineering is tech, so Cambridge made sense. Out of university he wrote code on the BBC iPlayer Android app, then managed investment portfolios, then went to McKinsey because he wanted to see inside as many industries as possible. At Bank of Georgia the transformation went so well the CEO asked him to join. He said yes. In 2021 he went back to startups to help Pensight grow, and in 2024 he went full-time on his own company.

Off the clock: bits of piano (Grade 8), lifting, the occasional hike, padel, and far more travel than most. Big foodie. London hidden gems: Brasserie Zédel, Dal Fiorentino, and a bar literally called “Bar with shapes as a name”. Works in English and Georgian; gets by in French.
