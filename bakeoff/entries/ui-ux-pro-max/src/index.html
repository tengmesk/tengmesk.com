04 — Point of view

AI is still underhyped. And overhyped.

Five things Tengo will tell you in the first ten minutes.

  1. AI is underhyped in the back office and overhyped in the boardroom.

    The boring workflows (invoices, reporting, ops) pay back first.

  2. Mid-sized companies have the AI advantage now.

    Fewer layers and faster decisions beat bigger budgets.

  3. Build vs buy has flipped.

    With AI, a custom internal tool often costs less than another 50 SaaS seats.

  4. The most expensive thing in tech isn't developers. It's slow decisions.

    Every week a decision waits, the price goes up and the window shrinks.

  5. Growth is an engineering problem now.

    The teams that win at go-to-market instrument everything and test weekly.
