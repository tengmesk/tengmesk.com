## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the trailhead board for Tengo's route. Every part of a trailhead already exists for a visitor who has to decide fast: the board states where you are and what this trail is, the fingerposts point at destinations with distances, the distance chart gives the numbers, the register holds what other walkers wrote. It refuses the personal-brand template (headshot right, headline left, logo row, three equal cards) and the hero-metric strip.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks on one paper. Deep forest green painted board (#12301F family) owns the hero, the proof board and the close; bone off-white (#F2ECDF) is the paper of notices pinned between them; brown timber (#5A3A1F) is every post, rule and frame, at true thickness (6–10px posts, 2px rules). Type: Besley (Clarendon revival; routed signage lettering) for every heading and number, Signika (a face designed for signage) for body and labels. Routed lettering sits cream on green; brown ink on bone. Components: boards (green, square-cornered, 2px cream inset rule like a routed border), fingerposts (arrow-ended boards on a post), the distance chart (ruled table), blazes (small painted rectangles), the register (ruled ledger lines), the photo print (cream-bordered rectangle set in a timber frame). Contour lines, drawn in code, as the board's ground in the hero and under the route. No grey, no gradients, no rounded cards.

STORY: In five seconds: this is Tengo Meskhi, he helps companies use tech and AI to grow faster and run leaner, he has walked every side of the table, and I can book a call right here. Scrolling: the fingerposts tell me where he can help; the distance chart and the register prove it with numbers and names; the route shows the career; the talks and the notes show the voice; the about shows the person; the close asks once more. Action: Book a call; LinkedIn second.

FIRST VIEWPORT (1440×900): a thin timber top rail with the routed name at left, four fingerpost links and the "Book a call" board at right. Below it the green trailhead board fills the viewport: faint contour lines across the ground; left 58%: the routed one-liner as h1 at ~64px over four lines, a three-line plain-words paragraph beneath, then two actions on one line, the cream "Book a 30-minute call" board (primary) and "LinkedIn" as a routed link; under them the "You are here" fingerpost: NOW · MIAMI, with BASE · LONDON beside it. Right 42%: the preferred headshot as a cream-bordered print set in a timber frame, slightly taller than the copy block, pinned to the board. The whole thing reads as one board, not a split hero: the frame, the post and the contour ground run behind both halves.

FORM: Trailhead board and waymarker system, candidate 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:3620dcd6b6b4df0c.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the "You are here" fingerpost. At rest it shows NOW · MIAMI. On hover, focus or tap the post grows three more fingers, COMING UP · SAN FRANCISCO · LOS ANGELES · ISTANBUL, each swinging out from the post (rotate from the post edge, exponential ease-out, 320ms, staggered 60ms); the state is sticky on tap and announced via aria-expanded. Motion grammar: things on this page are lit or pinned, never slid: boards backlight on hover (background steps one shade lighter), the career route draws on once as it enters the viewport, prints settle in with a 4px drop. Reduced motion: the fingers appear without swinging, the route is fully drawn, nothing fades.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
