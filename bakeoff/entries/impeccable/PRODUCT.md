consulting clients (via Conceptdigital), speaking invitations, partners/investors for the health venture,
board seats, media and hires, in that order. Success: a visitor books a call at https://cal.com/tengmesk/strategy;
secondary success: they connect on LinkedIn.

## Product Principles

1. Prove, don't claim: every assertion carries a number, a name, or a logo from the inputs.
2. One ask: booking a call is the single primary action, visible in every viewport.
3. Third person, plain words: write like Twain or Hormozi, cut the jargon, keep the wit.
4. Builder, not consultant: show shipped things and results before credentials.
5. Honesty over completeness: an empty feature is omitted or labelled, never faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

on hover, focus and tap; contrast at WCAG AA on the forest palette.
