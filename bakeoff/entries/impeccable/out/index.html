Trail notes. AI is still underhyped (and overhyped).

  • AI is underhyped in the back office and overhyped in the boardroom.

    The boring workflows, invoices, reporting, ops, pay back first.

  • Mid-sized companies have the AI advantage now.

    Fewer layers and faster decisions beat bigger budgets.

  • Build vs buy has flipped.

    With AI, a custom internal tool often costs less than another 50 SaaS seats.

  • The most expensive thing in tech isn't developers. It's slow decisions.

    Every week a decision waits, the estimate goes up and the window gets smaller.

  • Growth is an engineering problem now.

    The teams that win at go-to-market instrument everything and test weekly.

Off the trail.

Born and raised in Manchester to a Georgian family. Tengo is the short form of Tengiz, the name older press still uses. As a teenager he idolised Silicon Valley and sold sweets in the school playground to fund a World Challenge expedition to Argentina. That was the first business.

Engineering at Cambridge, because engineering is tech. First job: the BBC iPlayer Android app. Then finance, then McKinsey, because he wanted to see how every kind of business actually works from the inside. At Bank of Georgia the transformation went well enough that the CEO asked him to stay. In 2021 he went back to startups to help one grow.

Off the clock: piano (Grade 8, and a stint as first alto sax in national competition), lifting, the occasional long hike, padel, far more travel than most, and a serious appetite. He keeps a list of hidden gems. In London it starts with Brasserie Zédel, Dal Fiorentino, and a bar literally called "Bar with shapes as a name".

Three things he rates: curiosity, adventure, excellence.
